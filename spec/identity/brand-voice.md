# Brand Voice

This file is the compact operating summary. For the full machine-readable contract, use [TONE.md](./TONE.md).

## Voice

Reway sounds like a calm, precise personal tool for people who save often and need to find things again. It speaks to one person and their library, not to a team, company, or abstract productivity workflow. The voice is compact, capable, honest, and quietly opinionated.

Reway should feel useful to someone saving one tab between tasks and credible to someone rebuilding a large collection of research, references, or browser sessions.

## Tone

- **Serious:** Personal libraries deserve care. The product should not feel childish or disposable.
- **Casual:** Speak peer-to-peer, without corporate language or a manual-like register.
- **Respectful:** Assume the reader is capable; explain without patronizing.
- **Matter-of-fact:** Let speed, privacy, and retrieval speak for themselves. Do not inflate them.

## Prefer

- Lead with the outcome: save it, find it, see it, organize it when ready.
- Use concrete product language: bookmark, link, library, collection, tag, save, search.
- Use "you" and "your". Reway is a private, personal library.
- Keep headings declarative and short.
- Describe enrichment plainly when needed: the bookmark saves now; its details follow in the background.
- Make organization optional, not a burden or a prerequisite to saving.
- Describe visual retrieval through scanning, icons, images, and familiar references rather than vague productivity claims.
- Use "we" only for direct product statements or support communication.

## Avoid

- Do not sound like a cheap template.
- Do not sound like an inflated AI product.
- Do not call Reway a workspace, knowledge base, platform, or all-purpose productivity tool.
- Do not frame the product as a browser-folder replacement or criticise competitors by name.
- Do not promise impossible recall, perfect organization, or magical automation.
- Do not make background enrichment sound urgent, blocking, or user-controlled when it is not.
- Do not use corporate filler, generic superlatives, or launch-day excitement for ordinary actions.

## Copywriting Rules

1. Start with the reader's link, library, session, or retrieval need.
2. Name the outcome before the mechanism or technology.
3. Keep a save action immediate in the language as well as the product: no forms, ceremony, or implied delay.
4. Be exact about asynchronous behaviour and failure states without making them alarming.
5. Prefer short, active sentences. State what happened and what to do next.
6. Keep broad marketing surfaces stack-free; technical details belong in proof, process, capability, or developer-facing surfaces.
7. Use accessible, inclusive, sentence-case language across the website, dashboard, extension, and email.

## Messaging Boundaries

- Reway is a capture-first bookmark manager and personal library, not a general workspace.
- Canvas organizes and launches bookmark references; it is not a freeform whiteboard or diagramming tool.
- The product is private by default. Do not imply sharing, collaboration, or public discovery unless that capability exists.
- AI is not part of Reway's brand pitch. Metadata enrichment is a quiet product mechanism, not intelligence marketing.
- Website copy remains reader-centred and outcome-first.
- Technical stack details are proof after the outcome is clear, never the primary pitch.

## Example Direction

> Save the tab you're on. It's in your library.
>
> Reway keeps your links together, easy to scan, and ready when you need them.
